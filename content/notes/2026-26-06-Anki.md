Anki is a spaced repetition app/website/mcp

Remember who you are

## Why
### learning is memory. 
- To build novel ideas, you need to fetch the base ideas into cache, operate on them, and then store the result on top. If the base ideas are not recalled, the novel idea cannot be formed.
> Our claims of “learning is not memorization” may sound poetic in the abstract, but when asked to break them down concretely, we find our explanations start to sound more and more like “tower-building is not nailing wood, not welding steel, not balancing load, it is none of those mechanical things, it is the emergent grace of brushing the heavens.” Which is completely devoid of instructional value, and entirely unhelpful to any aspiring tower-builder, aside from possibly inspiring a fleeting spark of motivation that spends itself searching for a place to spend itself.
### Memories decay
- The probability of recalling something decays exponentially with time. 
- 80% of what you learn today will be forgotten by the next week.
- forgetting_curves.jpg

### How
- intentionality in practice. 
- retrieval effort. Higher the effort put into recalling something, higher the chances of the neuronal pathways being forged.

### Setup
- claude desktop + anki desktop + ankimcp to create cards
- Ankidroid + anki desktop - practice anywhere 
- flag and edit bad cards, or with cards that represent concepts with more clarity - elicits the feeling of something opaque rotating to become transparent
- daily routine - morning - 5-10 cards - effortful recall - 3 minutes maximum
- supports voice, latex, html, images
