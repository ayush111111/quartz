---
title: "Upskilling is Hard and That's a Good Thing"
date: 2026-06-23
---

> Upskilling takes work. That's a feature, not a bug. Upskilling increases agency and outsized agency is best held in the hands of people who know what it means to put in outsized work. We all know what happens when lazy people have power. The fact that upskilling takes work is a force against that.

— Justin Skycak
