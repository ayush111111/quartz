Communication determines the success of every relationship — personal, professional, and with yourself.

## Overview

- Keep calm, listen, speak your truth
- Prepare for conversations as much as you can
- Anxiety is lack of clarity — plan conversations to fight and confront communication gaps aggressively
- Defer conversations to the next day if you are not in a mental state to have the conversation

## With LLMs

LLM slop is anti-communication. Communication should convey ideas simply and effectively. It doesn't need to be long paragraphs.
